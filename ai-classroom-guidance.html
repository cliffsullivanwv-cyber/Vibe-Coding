AI Classroom Guidance

Purpose

This guidance helps educators use artificial intelligence ethically and effectively in the classroom.

Key Principles

  • Promote responsible use of AI tools.
  • Protect student data and privacy.
  • Encourage transparency in AI-supported learning.
  • Use AI to support learning, not replace teacher judgment.

Suggested Practices

  • Introduce AI tools with clear learning goals.
  • Discuss how AI works and its limitations.
  • Review outputs critically with students.
  • Focus on collaboration, creativity, and digital citizenship.
